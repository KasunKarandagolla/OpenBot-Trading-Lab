# 02-multi-timeframe-structure — v2.0.0-research

Independently read clean 4h/1h/15m/5m views before targeted overlays. Map trend/range/transition, swing progression, location and impulse/pullback. Explain whether lower-TF action is continuation, a potential reversal, a separate range play or unresolved conflict. A bearish 4h context does not alone settle a lower-TF hypothesis. State strongest contrary evidence, plausible alternative and exact falsification. EMA crosses are not structural breaks. Current chart pivots are strict 2L/2R with confirmation at i+2 close availability; frozen recipe pivots may differ and require their own version/hash. Future bars cannot relabel earlier-known structure.
