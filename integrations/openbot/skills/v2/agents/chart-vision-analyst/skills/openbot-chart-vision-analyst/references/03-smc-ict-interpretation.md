# 03-smc-ict-interpretation — v2.0.0-research

SMC/ICT labels are descriptive hypotheses. A sweep needs a boundary known before excursion, actual breach, completed reclaim and the frozen recipe's confirmation. A wick alone is insufficient. BOS/CHoCH requires a pre-known swing and declared close rule, never EMA order. FVG needs three completed bars and detection time. Order block/equal highs/premium-discount need explicit definitions/range/tolerance available at cutoff. No hidden institutions, stop-hunting or liquidation intent inferred from drawings. Strong impulse through a boundary can favor continuation despite a marginal reclaim; examine both paths and nearest obstacles.
