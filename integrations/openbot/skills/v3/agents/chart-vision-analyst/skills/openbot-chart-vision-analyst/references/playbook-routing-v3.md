# Market reading and playbook judgment — v3

## Read the market before choosing a recipe

Make a 4H/1H map: directional structure and its confirmation time; balance or rotation; volatility versus the recent distribution; price at/near/far from pre-known support/resistance; expansion versus corrective participation; BTC alignment if sourced; nearest obstacles. Keep these dimensions separate. EMA order is context, not BOS. A bullish 5m bounce under a bearish 4H is a possible short rally-failure, possible early reversal, or range rotation: describe the exact discriminating observation instead of voting the timeframes.

First write two falsifiable hypotheses. For a resistance boundary, long acceptance/retest and short sweep/reclaim may both be watchable. Neither requires all of VWAP, sweep, BOS, FVG and OTE. A tool calculates their evidence; a researcher explains which premise matters. Far-from-level price may be INTERESTING but has no watchable geometry until a meaningful location forms. Missing flow blocks only a policy actually requiring genuine flow, not every OHLCV playbook.

## Breakout / acceptance / retest continuation

Boundary must predate excursion: prior completed range, day boundary or confirmed HTF pivot. Freeze boundary and opposing failure thesis. Two completed closes outside are the v3 acceptance policy, not a universal truth. Check displacement, renewed participation and available target room; avoid chasing an expanded bar. Retest variant watches a later touch and close on the accepted side, with bounded age; immediate comparator acts only after causal acceptance and has a separate native run. Retest can miss runaway winners or fill adverse reversals; do not presume superiority.

Structural stop is outside the retest low/high and the acceptance boundary with an ATR buffer. First credible known obstacle caps target; a farther desired R target cannot erase nearer resistance/support. Acceptance lost on two closes is a thesis exit; protective stop, five-minute order expiry, no-chase and maximum hold run mechanically. Re-arming requires a new frozen setup identity; never rewrite an expired one.

Counterexample: a high-volume wick above resistance closes inside. It is not accepted breakout. Another: accepted breakout occurs but price is already far above boundary and next resistance is close; WATCH or reject economics even if trend is strong. An unclear retest can remain developing without a forced entry.

## Sweep / reclaim / failed-breakout reversal

Identify prior boundary and how/when it was known. Require excursion through it, completed reclaim into the old range, then the declared later confirmation (or a separately versioned early-reclaim comparator). Compare genuine acceptance in the breakout direction. Small reclaim against fresh expanding HTF impulse is a contradiction, not automatic proof of reversal. A mature impulse at a known outer boundary can justify WATCH even before BOS confirms; avoid indiscriminate countertrend veto.

Stop goes beyond the full excursion extreme plus volatility buffer. The range midpoint and nearest opposing confirmed level are credible objectives only if pre-known and beyond entry. They can make the plan uneconomic. A wick or volume spike does not prove a stop hunt, institutional order block, absorption or liquidations. When close stays outside, route continuation instead. Lower-TF confirmation must be later than setup arming; do not use the same bar twice as setup and future confirmation.

Counterexample: boundary selected after seeing wick; reject causal thesis. A sweep returning inside with sound geometry is still only researchable; original Risk and current gate remain required for native qualification. Losing after a valid process is not proof that the initial decision was mistaken.

## Trend / VWAP and HTF reactions

Preserve VWAPTrendContinuationV1 as a frozen control. Controlled pullback, meaningful trend support, renewed participation and logical stop matter; crossing VWAP alone in opposing displacement is insufficient. State quote/base versus typical-price VWAP, UTC anchor, warm-up and missing-session status. No silent feature substitution into a control.

HTF reaction is a context/opportunity family: a pre-confirmed support/resistance zone, its defense/rejection, room for rotation or continuation and competing failure thesis. It may route either native challenger. An independent native HTF strategy is NOT claimed unless actually implemented and run. Midrange rotation with little room can be correct NO_TRADE. Support is a region, not immunity to a break.


Targets follow a declared timeframe, structural role and lifecycle. The registered v3.0.2 policy uses still-unconsumed, relevant-side 1h/4h pivots and declared range levels. A completed close through a pivot consumes its old role; a later role flip needs a new explicit hypothesis. Do not erase older unbroken obstacles or retain every already-broken pivot as an eternal wall. Report lower-timeframe obstacles as cautions, and investigate them, without silently introducing a new hard objective veto into the frozen policy. A different objective map requires its own version and comparison.

## Supporting SMC / ICT evidence

BOS closes through a pre-known level; CHoCH changes a prior established direction. Specify timing and wick/close rule. FVG needs three completed bars and a gap definition; inverse FVG requires prior gap failure then the reversal rule. Order block requires declared source candle, subsequent qualifying impulse, known_at and mitigation; do not choose it after the outcome. OTE/premium-discount depends on a causal dealing range, not a range selected to fit the winner. These are contextual/experimental components unless a separately frozen native policy consumes them. No proprietary label proves edge.

## Economic and management judgment

Use actual invalidation and volatility before sizing. `risk=notional*(stop_fraction+costs)`, margin=notional/leverage; leverage does not shrink cash price risk at fixed notional. Record gross stop-R and cost-inclusive R separately. Entry expiry is not max holding time. No fill from the signal close: native execution begins on a subsequent executable candle; limit touch/through are assumptions, queue/partials unknown. Fees are explicit; funding needs timestamped sourced rates/marks; spread/slippage are stress scenarios where no contemporaneous book exists.

Nearest obstacle is checked before desired R. Stop >2.5% is STRUCTURAL_STOP_POLICY_CONFLICT; retain wider-stop/reduced-notional hypothesis separately without weakening the gate. Stop inside normal noise is not repaired by increasing leverage. Fixed full exits are M0; partials, breakeven and trailing need paired same-entry experiments including harmful exits and added costs. A small positive cohort is UNQUALIFIED; negative and nonfilled cases belong in the report.

## Vision and challenge procedure

Ask purposeful source-sealed chart questions: does a later 5m reaction support the boundary already seen on 1h; is an apparent 15m reclaim actually acceptance on 4h; is a nearby obstacle omitted? Inspect pixels before visual claims, bind request/image/source hashes and visible cutoff, then report strongest contradiction. If only URL/text arrives, vision_pixels_seen=false and UNQUALIFIED; never fabricate native attachment receipts. Local Codex visual review is separate from original OpenBot Vision.

Director coordinates original specialists, preserves challenges/rebuttal and reconciles a single source/cutoff. A numerical PASS cannot supply the missing original Risk role. Missing account/spec/funding/news data are named unknowns. NO_TRADE is valid, but examine the alternative playbook and developing opportunity before concluding nothing merits watching.
