# 03 — Causal SMC/ICT as descriptive hypotheses, not mystical certainty

## Strict evidence hierarchy

The Lab already has causal confirmed 2-left/2-right swings, BOS/CHoCH, timestamped boundaries, displacement and FVG research modules. Reuse those outputs as **verified numerical context** where present. A screenshot can propose a location but does not independently prove confirmation time or exact level.

## Terms and proof requirements

- **Confirmed swing:** pivot visible only after required right-hand candles close. A pivot drawn retroactively at its extreme is NOT a signal available at that extreme. If confirmation timestamp absent, say `visual candidate swing, unconfirmed`.
- **BOS:** a declared, previously known structural swing is broken in the continuation direction by the explicitly stated close/wick rule. Require level, rule and timestamp. Do not equate any EMA break with BOS.
- **CHoCH / MSS:** a structural change against the currently established swing sequence; require causal reference swing and observation cutoff; not a guarantee of reversal.
- **Liquidity sweep / raid:** price exceeds an objectively predefined prior extreme, then returns/rejects under an objective rule. A long wick alone is insufficient; neither a sweep nor a wick proves stop orders or institutional manipulation.
- **Displacement:** abnormally directional expansion versus a defined recent-bar baseline (range/body/ATR and optionally volume), not merely an impressive screenshot candle. Exact threshold needs numerical verification.
- **FVG:** 3-candle gap/imbalance under a specified three-bar high/low definition with completed timestamps; filter poor-quality small gaps and state whether it was subsequently mitigated only using data available at the cutoff.
- **Order block:** discretionary source-dependent construct; define last opposing candle, impulse, valid structure and mitigation before applying it. No claim that institutions actually entered there.
- **Equal highs/lows:** level clustering with explicit price tolerance; image appearance may be sensitive to zoom.
- **Premium/discount:** price relative to a declared causal range; do not select range after knowing the result.

## Verification sequence for a proposed LONG sweep reversal

1. Locate the **pre-existing** swing low/day low/range boundary and identify when it became known.
2. Prove excursion beyond that low under the Lab recipe; identify the observed low and reclaim close.
3. Check whether the supposed reclaim is only marginal and within an active bearish impulse; it may be **continuation** rather than a genuine failed move.
4. Examine nearby realistic targets: range midpoint, VWAP, opposing level; account for overhead obstacles and the specified 4h holding cap.
5. Verify invalidation beyond excursion low with appropriate ATR buffer, rather than moving a stop for convenient RR.
6. Return `CANDIDATE`, `WATCH`, or `UNQUALIFIED`—not an image-only trade authorization.

Mirror for SHORT upper-boundary failures. Note that a structure filter can reject winners as well as losers: record this explicitly in paired simulations. Exact SMC/ICT vocabulary varies across teachers; identify definitions before mixing methods.

## Common red flags

Retrospective pivot rewriting; FVG visible only after the proposed decision; 'liquidity grab' inferred from wick alone; choosing best levels after seeing price; mixing hours of live-chart changes into a single same-second state; inventing win probabilities; declaring a particular teacher's strategy universally profitable.
