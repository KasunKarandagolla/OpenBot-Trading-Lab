## What the measured results support

The native October signed arm admits three sweep plans versus one cost-matched
control plan. It loses **0.23436884 USDT** versus the control's **+0.50352528**.
More coverage was economically harmful in this development sample. Regime
matching and removal of the signed selection filter produce the same three
fills; they demonstrate no independent economic improvement here.

The structure arm removes two October 29 continuation-danger long reversals in
ETH/XRP, both control losses, leaving the October 2 XRP short winner. Its
**+0.50352528** is one trade, not an edge or a promotion decision. Keep the
rejected cases and test whether this is useful boundary evidence or overfiltering
on independent chronological data. Do not optimize the boundary tolerance against
these outcomes.

The profile and structure-objective fallback arms make no observed economic
change. Automatic nearest-objective veto removes the sole winner and retains the
two losers, worsening net to **-0.73789412**. Retain this concrete harmful-veto
example. A nearby obstacle must not silently become a universal veto.

The 0.1ATR retracement entry arm keeps three modeled fills and improves net by
**0.10552990** to **-0.12883894**. It remains negative. Its frozen stop is retained
and fixed 2R is recomputed from the new entry, so this is an entry/geometry-plan
comparison, not a pure price-only attribution with the old target held constant.
15m range fills cannot establish queue priority or prove an intra-bar fill occurred
before five-minute expiry. Preregister finer-data fill/expiry qualification before
promoting that observation.

There are ten parent VWAP and 24 parent sweep signals in the common population;
no new VWAP plan passes the tested admission/routing geometry. The independent
frozen VWAP portfolio has nine native fills and negative net. Continuation
selection uplift is therefore **insufficiently evidenced**, not successful merely
because those entries disappeared. The 0.0038 allowance plus net-RR floor is a
dominant coverage constraint. Keep it consistent and disclose any future change
as a new hypothesis rather than tuning October until it trades.
