## Discovery quality and external dependencies

The ordinary runner no longer caps review at one card, operational history at
18+2 coins, question investigation at six facts, or current reference analysis at
4–12 objects. It retains protected cross-sectional audits and symmetrically
allows contrary relative cases. Work is bounded by actual transport/retry/time/
byte/freshness budgets. All deferred nominees stay visible; inventory519 never
means519 deeply investigated contracts. No typical 3–6 outcome becomes a quota;
the approved output cap of five research coins remains, and alternatives are
explicitly unranked rather than falsely rejected.

Independent specialist rights/feeds, primary M14/M15 definitions, actual fees and
future execution liquidity, sustainable source quota, new provider schemas,
real model interpretation quality and prospective recall/performance remain
external/source-dependent. Genuine zero research results are allowed; missing
coverage or infrastructure must be named. No orders, trading credentials,
Freqtrade/admin/database changes, scheduling, new agents or model reassignment.
